Residential — Kota Baru Parahyangan, Bandung — 2022

IS House

620 m² organised around a shaded courtyard. Thirty-one photographs and a four-minute film, in the order the house is walked.

The brief

A house with no corridors, cool at noon.

We drew a single 32-metre volume, cut a courtyard into the middle of it, and let the roof overhang deep enough that the glass never needs a curtain.

Every fitted element — the 7-metre kitchen bench, the library wall, the beds — was drawn by the studio and built by one joinery workshop in Yogyakarta.
